## Process Summary

OctoAcme manages projects through an iterative lifecycle. Teams initiate work by validating the business need, success measures, stakeholders, and go/no-go decision. Approved initiatives move into planning, where teams define prioritized increments, acceptance criteria, owners, milestones, risks, and dependencies.

During execution, teams track work on a project board, coordinate through regular team and stakeholder communication, review progress, test changes, and escalate blockers through defined paths. The Project Manager coordinates delivery, risks, and communications; the Product Manager owns outcomes and prioritization; Developers build and test; QA validates acceptance and quality; and stakeholders provide input and approvals.

Releases require acceptance criteria to be met, passing CI and security checks, release notes, smoke tests, and rollback or mitigation readiness. Teams verify deployments and communicate releases to stakeholders. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents capture lessons and assign improvement actions with owners and due dates. Clear ownership, customer value, data-informed decisions, and continuous learning guide the process throughout.
